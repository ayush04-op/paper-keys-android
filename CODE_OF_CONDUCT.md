# Code of Conduct

Paper Keys is a community project. Participants are expected to help keep its spaces welcoming, constructive, and safe for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, respectful, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and behavior, not people.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.
- Respect privacy; do not publish another person’s personal or sensitive information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized attention without consent, doxxing, and sustained disruptive conduct are not acceptable in project discussions or contributions.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub’s contact/reporting tools. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.
